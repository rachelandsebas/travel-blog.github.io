# Lunch and Turano Lake

This day the spouse tour took me to Turano Lake, a beautiful lake about 1.5 hours away from Rome. Our first stop was lunch which was delicious. They kept bringing more and more food and each dish was more delicious than the last. I was sadly way too full to finish. After lunch, we visited the lake. I was able to both go down to the water and cross the bridge to go up to another cute medieval city.

# The conference gala

The conference hosted a gala at a film studio that is used for Roman-style films. The decoration was great and they had performances including opera and dances. The opera was primarily well-known opera songs, so Sebastian enjoyed it.

We spent less than an hour here as Axel (coworker) decided to ditch and go to a restaurant. Food at the hotel wasn't great and he wanted to have real Italian food.
