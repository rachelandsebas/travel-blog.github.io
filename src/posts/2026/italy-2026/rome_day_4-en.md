# Tuscany and a medieval village

While Sebastian attended the conference, Rachel got to go on the conference-organized spouse tour. The first day of the conference we went to a vineyard in Tuscany for lunch. I was very excited until I realized Tuscany is 2.5 hours away from Rome. After a long bus drive, we made it to the restaurant and had pasta, polenta, and got to try some of their home-grown wine. After lunch, we visited a nearby medieval village. It was super cute with beautiful stone streets.
