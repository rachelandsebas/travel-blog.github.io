# Galleria dell’Accademia

Our first stop in Florence was the Galleria dell’Accademia where we got to see Michelangelo's David and lots of cool paintings. For many paintings, seeing them in person (for non-artsy people like us) is not too different from seeing a photo online. However, for a sculpture like the David, it was really cool to see it in person. It is 5+ meters tall and the details were exquisite.

# Santa Maria del Fiore

After the museum we went to the Basilica de Santa Maria del Fiore which hosts Florence’s iconic dome. They began building the church in the late 1200s and knew that they wanted to build a gigantic dome. However, at the time they didn't have the technology to do so. Regardless, they began building the rest of the church, confident that eventually they would figure it out. About 200 years later, they finally found someone who could build the dome and successfully finished the church.

# Basilica di Santa Croce

Our next stop was the Basilica di Santa Croce. Built by Franciscan monks in the 1200s, this church is the burial place of many of Italy's greats. The floor was covered with tombs. Along the sides of the room were the tombs and monuments of people like Machiavelli, Galileo Galilei, Michelangelo, Leonardo Da Vinci, Dante.
