# A private pasta class

Our activity for today was a cooking class! We booked a group class but only we had booked for today so it ended up being a private class. Our chef was a linguist by training and speaks five languages. We booked the class in English but he realized we spoke Spanish too, so he gave the class in Spanish (he was tired of speaking English all week).  The class was a pasta class. We made pasta noodles from scratch and four different pasta sauces: cacio e pepe, amatriciana, carbonara, and gricia. Amatriciana was our favourite!

Let's just say Sebastian struggled with making the pasta noodles 😅, but we were impressed with how simple yet delicious they all were. We'll be making them back home but Rachel will make the noodles. There was a sous chef who also helped out with the class. She spoke Italian and some English. It was fun because Italian and Spanish are so similar, so she spoke Italian to us and we would reply in Spanish.
