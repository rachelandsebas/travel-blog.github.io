Our first stop was the Spanish Steps. Rome seems a little...culturally confused. We visited the Spanish Steps, which were built by the French kings because it is close to the Vatican. At the top of the steps was an Egyptian obelisk. And all this was in Italy.

# The Pantheon

Then we visited the Pantheon. This site was a Roman Temple since 27BC, then this building was built as a new temple in 118AD. It was converted into a church in 609AD, and they still run services there!
