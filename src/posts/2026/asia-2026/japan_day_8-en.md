# Shibuya Crossing

We had some time before our next activity, so we went to Shibuya Crossing, the Times Square of Tokyo.

We also got to see the Hachiko statue, which is a statue of a dog that waited for his owner at the train station every day for years, even after his owner passed away.

# Sake Market

Sake is a Japanese alcoholic beverage made from fermented rice. We found a place near where we were that offered all you can drink for 30 minutes for $15. This was a very fun way to try lots of different drinks for a very reasonable price. Sebastián liked the fruit sakes, and I liked anything sweet.
