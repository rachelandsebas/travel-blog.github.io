# Great Wall of China

Today we saw the Great Wall of China! This was Sebastián's third Wonder of the World and my first.

It's crazy how old the wall is. The first section were from the 600s BC. The section we were at was original built in the 500s AD. Renovations have been ongoing since with a major rebuild in the 1300s.

We took a cable car up to the wall and walked along the wall from tower to tower. They say that you don't _hike_ the Great Wall, you _climb_ it, and that's accurate! The whole thing is stairs and slopes - some parts are gentle slopes and others an almost vertical staircase. We climbed from tower 14 up to tower 22. Tower 20 is called the Hero's Tower because it is difficult to climb to - so we were very proud to make it there and pass. The views from the wall were georgeous (once the fog cleared) and we have definitely gained a renewed respect for those who built the wall and the soldiers who had to walk along it.

# Summer Palace

We did the Great Wall with a tour group, and after the Wall, we travelled together to the Summer Palace. The palace was originally built by an Emperor for his mother as a 60th birthday gift. He dugged out a lake in the shape of a peach, used a dirt to create a hill, and built her a palace on the hill. (To our moms: we love you both a lot, but please don't expect this type of gifts from us!)

At the palace, we took a boat ride across the lake, climbed the temple, then tour the palace and heard stories of those who live there (google Empress Cixi, the Dragon Lady, if you want crazy stories).

The area is beautiful and peaceful and was one of my favourite place we have been.

# Dinner

As some of you may know, we have a scratch off map on our wall and we are trying to eat food from every country in the world. We saw that Beijing had a North Korean restaurant and had planned to go there as getting North Korean is quite challenging. However, we realized last minute that the restaurant chain is managed and operated by the North Korean goverment and it's revenue is used to fund the current regimen so we decided not to go.

In it's place, we found a highly recommended Michelin guide noodle restaurant that was on the way to our restaurant. After dinner, we walked around and got some fruity drinks and dessert (Tanghulu, candied fruits).
