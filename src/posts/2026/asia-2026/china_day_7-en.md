The day Leaders would like to avoid was upon us. Today was the start of problem coordination. This is when we defend our students' solutions and final grades based on the grading schemes the intentional jury chose.

Pablo (Ecuador's Deputy Leader) and I stayed up very late going over students' solutions to determine how many points to ask for and understand where we would have to fight for the marks. Unfortunately we were scheduled to coordinate problems 1 and 4 (the easier ones) first thing in the morning, which meant we had less time to prepare for the problems we would argue the most about. 

We managed to coordinate most problems today, but, as expected, the conversations coordination for problems 1 and 4 didn't finish in our alloted time so we will continue those tomorrow. Let's just say there is one student with a 38 page solution that should be grateful I found a way to piece together his solution in a more coherent way, lol.

After a grueling day of coordination, we had a chill night after dinner so I was able to catch up on posts, have a bath, a short nap, and went over the solutions again in preparation for tomorrow's coordinations.
