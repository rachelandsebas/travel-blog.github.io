## Interesting Facts

Warning: This is my recollection of the guide's commentary. Believe it at your own risk (or fact check).

1. North Korea and South Korea are still technically at war. The Korean War ended in 1953 with an armistice, not a peace treaty. The two countries are still in a state of war.
2. 4 tunnels have been discovered that North Korea has dug to try to invade South Korea. They were between 1974 and 1990. There is no clear evidence of how long it took to dig them, or if those are all, but it is clear from remains that there was intent to invade South Korea. 30000 soldiers could have come through each tunnel with their weapons in 1 hour. Not clear how long they took to build, but with the technology at the time, it would have taken a long time.
3. The UN has a presence in the DMZ. They have a small base in the area and are responsible for monitoring the armistice. Because of this, tourism at the DMZ is also regulated by the UN. So decisions around photography and access have been changed recently by them due to the current tensions in the area. In fact, you could tour the JSA (Joint Security Area) before, but now you can't.
4. Both still provoke each other in other ways. They blast speakers with propaganda (North Korea) or K-pop (South Korea) at the DMZ. NK also jams signal and sends trash balloons to the South.
5. Having been to South Korea on its own makes it very unlikely you could ever visit North Korea (already hard in the first place).
