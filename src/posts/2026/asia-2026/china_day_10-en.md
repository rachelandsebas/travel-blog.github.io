## Xinzha Road

My greatgranparents were missionaries in China in the 1930s and 40s. The headquarters of the organization they were with were in Shanghai, but the buildings were surrender when the organization had to leave China in the 1950s. Before I left for China, my grandma was telling me the stories and showed me old photos. I was able to find the buildings - they are still standing!

## A lesson learned

On my way back to the hotel, it started to rain. At first it was just a few drops, so I continued on to the hotel hoping to drop my bags before going out for dinner. I noticed all the locals starting to dash for cover in nearby shops, but since I didn't mind getting a little wet and I had a raincoat, I continued on to my hotel which was not far away. 

Within minutes it was no longer just drops and became pouring. I was also having trouble with my maps and kept thinking I was just a block or two away from my hotel, so I continued on taking cover under roofs whenever possible. The rain kept getting worse and worse. Eventually I realized there was no point on continuing on, and decided to go into the nearest restaurant to get dinner before going back. 

I saw what looked like a restaurant and entered only to realize it was a massage parlore. The workers there were super sweet and brought me paper towels and padded me dry. They gave me a cup of hot tea and offered me a massage. I waited there for a few minutes until there was a pause in the rain and then dashed back to my hotel. I arrived soaked from head to toe and decided that in the future when I see locals going for cover I should follow their lead.
