# Sebastian's afternoon

## Closing Ceremony

Similar to the opening ceremony, we had wonderful performances. Kids from different ages performed dances from each continent. There was also a bit of opera and orchestra. We had our honorable mentions shown on the stage as well seeing all the medallists go to the stage to receive their medals (and a stuffed panda). Additionally, we congratulated 2 exceptional achievements: a student who got a perfect sccored for the second time in a row and a student topped the IMO Hall of Fame with 5 golds and 2 silvers in 7 participations.

## Farewell Party

After the ceremony, we headed for dinner at the main cafeteria of Shanghai High School. There were a lot of games and activities (art & craft) where the students got a lot of souveniers and prizes. They also put enough of kiosks with dessets and snacks to have an entire 7-course dinner after dinner, lol.

## Wine :)

A bunch of the Latin American Leaders got together after the student's party to have some wine and conversations about our national math olympiads. It was great to hear how, even with limited resources, we are all trying to do the best for our students. With this I said goodbye to them and the IMO. Looking forward to the next one!
