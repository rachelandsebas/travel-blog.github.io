After breakfast we headed to TeamLab Boarderless - a digital art museum. It far exceeded our expectations. The museum is a series of rooms with different projections on the wall - flowers, birds, characters, etc. The projections move (even between rooms) and change, so you can sit in one room for a while and watch the different animations.

One of the rooms was an ocean that was filled with animals colored by the visitors! In this room you can choose an animal to color and later your paper is scanned and the resulting drawing becomes a new animal being displayed in the room. It's impressive that the animations also performed facial gestures such as winking. The room was also interactive, so you could touch the animals and the would move away or turn around.

# Omakase

For dinner we experienced an omakase! This is a Japanese dining style that translates to "I'll leave it up to you". No à la carte just trust the chef as he makes the different dishes in front of you. Over the course of 2 hours, the chef prepared different types of sushi and side dishes for us to eat.

This was great way to try high quality sushi and some dishes we would not have ordered otherwise. The food was amazing and extremely fresh.
