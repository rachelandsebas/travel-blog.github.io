# Lunch

Bibimbap was a must in my list of things to try in Korea. It felt wrong to come all the way here and not find out what it tastes like here. So I dediced I would try to get it today. I noticed that the traditional North American version, is more of the homemade/base version of bibimbap. But, at least in Seoul, restaurants don't have the base version. I found a nice restaurant nearby that had Yukhoe (think beef tartare) bibimbap with Cheonggukjang (spicy fermented soybean paste soup with beef) on the side. It was pretty good!

# Naksan Park Cats

At the top of the trail, we found 2 cats. They were quite cute and clearly they went together everywhere. They were not scared of people either. On our way out, we accidentally took the wrong trail and had to cut through a residential area to get back to the main trail. I found a a building that had two statutes of cats on the roof. They were the same cats!! It turns out it is a cat cafe (didn't go in, not sure if just branding or if they actually have cats there), so those cats are probably there all the time. So cool!

# Dinner

We took an Uber from the bottom of the trail to a restaurant. On the way we picked up a colleague at his hotel. We've known that Uber is really mostly used by foreigners in Korea, so some drivers are less familiar with the app. This time we had set in the app that we wanted to make a stop at the hotel and then go to the restaurant. The driver thought there was only one stop. In fact, our colleague got into the car and the driver didn't even notice as he was still explaining (in Korean) that we needed to get off. I pulled out my translator app while all of this was happenning as he was saying "Mistake? Mistake?" thinking that we last minute change the destination. I said "No, no, we needed to pick up our friend", pointing to my colleague in the back. When it finished translating, he was shocked that there was an extra person in the car and said something like "I thought you made a dumb mistake."

Food was great, similar to how we cook pork in Ecuador. After that someone suggested to go to a fancy cocktail bar. I was hesitant but supposedly one of the best in Korea so I said why not. We got there and we were 18th in the queue... Decided to walk around the area (very nice) but no progress on getting in, so we just went back to our hotels.
