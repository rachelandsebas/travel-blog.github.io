# Dinner

Had a nice dinner at a Korean BBQ place with everyone who came from TD. I thought the tradition at Korean BBQ was to cook the meat yourself, but this place had a people that cooked it for us. Apparently that's the true fancy way to do it. The food was absolutely amazing, and so were the conversations (and the soju, lol).
