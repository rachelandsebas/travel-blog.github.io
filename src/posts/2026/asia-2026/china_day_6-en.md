# Taxis

Before coming to China, Rachel and I did some research on best ways to pay for stuff and how to order a cab. We setup Alipay and Wechat for this. Some of the kids that came had done so as well. Unfortunately, for most people additionally setup still require getting SMS verification codes that were no longer possible to get here. Thankfully my setup was (initially fine), so I was able to get a cab on our way to the Tower. However, my account was flagged as suspicious and could no longer do it afterwards without doing a full in-person verification of my account. On our way back we had to get taxis on the street that clearly wanted to scam us by charging 3-4x the regular price. Finally we had to accept a 30% higher rate since we needed to get the kids back to the dorms before curfew. When haggling over the price with the driver, we were using a translator app and I said "This ride is X% higher than what DiDi is showing", to what he replied "But you can't get that right now, can you?" which I thought was hilarious and eventually agreed on something more reasonable.
