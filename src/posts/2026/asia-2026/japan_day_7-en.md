# Ueno Zoo

From there, we visited the Ueno Zoo. Sebastián has been wanting to see a panda (we didn't see one in China as the zoos we were close to were not very ethical), but unfortunately Ueno Zoo returned their pandas to China a few months ago. Hilariously, they still have the panda enclosures set up exactly the same, except instead of real pandas, they had cardboard cutouts. Sebastián was happy to see red pandas at least!

I really liked watching the elephants. We saw one playing with a tire and got to see him have a snack and a drink.

## Akihabara

Akihabara is a huge shopping district for all things anime. Sebastián was specially excited to see the Pokémon section - he watched a lot of it growing up.

It was very hard to choose just a few of the Pokémon stuffed animals to buy, but he was able to narrow it down to three, lol.

Sebastián also has declared one of the Pokémon, Psyduck, to be my spirit Pokémon because it always has headaches and covers his ears when overwhelmed...just like me. So he bought me a Psyduck!
