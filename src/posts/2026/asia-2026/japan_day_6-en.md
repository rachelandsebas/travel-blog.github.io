# Breakfast

For breakfast, we went a small cafe right beside the hotel. It was a more western style breakfast. As expected, the menu was in japanese, but there were pictures. We saw a bagel sandwich (with bacon and egg). When we translated it, the translation said donut bacon egg sandwich, and I thought that was a funny mistranslation of bagel as we have seen many hilarious mistranslations. When my sandwich arrived, it was in fact a donut sandwich! Sebastián ordered a pizza toast and milk coffee. It was delicious.

This was also the first time I have seen a fork since arriving in Asia a week and a half ago, lol. Suffice to say my chopstick skills have improved significantly!

# Dog Cafe

Then we went to a small dog cafe. They had 4 rescued dogs that are up for adoption. We had a lot of fun playing with the dogs. One in particular, Mitora, a 4-month-old puppy, was highly energetic and loved playing fetch with Sebastián. He was also highly adept at untying shoelaces and on multiple times was able to grab the end of Sebastián's shoelace and pull, untying it.

Ritcho, a 7-year-old, was very fluffy and enjoyed being scratched. After I had been padding one side of her for a couple of minutes, she turned around showing I should pad the other side. The other two dogs were much more shy so we didn't get to play with them as much.

# Heading back to the hotel

Exiting the train station, an 8-minute walk from our hotel, it was starting to rain a bit. Thankfully we had an umbrella so we were happy to walk. But then we saw lightning and heard the loudest thunder either of us have heard in our lives, and it started to pour. We found cover and hid to wait out the worst of the storm. Then it started hailing, big chunks of hail. Eventually the rain and lighting slowed enough that we were able to dash the last few minutes back to our hotel.
