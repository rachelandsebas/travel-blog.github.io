# Coordination

Final day of coordination! After an exhausting day yesterday coordinating most problems, we were scheduled to come back to the coordination room for a few student/problems whose scores we didn't agree on. We prepared for a grueling fight. There was one student in particular with very long solutions to two problems (28 and 37 pages, respectively). While his solutions were a bit messy, there were complete solutions, so I was willing to take the fight all the way up to the international jury if needed. Thankfully we didn't have to raise this disagreements further up than the problem captain and we completed coordination in an amicable way. Needless to say, we have since told this student he needs to work on organizing his thoughts and solutions.

# Shopping Mall

The kids felt like they were trapped. To be fair, the security around the campus and the long procedure to get them out has surprised me a lot compared to previous editions of this competitions. Because of this, we took them out to the nearest shopping mall to get dinner and buy some snacks/souvenirs. I also got them all to try Yakult! Even though yakult is Japanese, I saw at the store and figured that it was a must try while we were there.

On our way back, we all got CoCo! there was a sweet gentleman who helped our students with their orders. However, this stop ended being not so smart. It started pouring quite badly.
