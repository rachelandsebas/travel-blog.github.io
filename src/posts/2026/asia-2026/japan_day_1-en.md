# Osaka

With minor hiccups we were able to get from the aiport to downtown Osaka. We are missing how easy it was to get around the city with perfectly labelled signs in China. We are hoping the rest of Japan is better, lol. We only planned to be there for the afternoon, so we knew we were not going to be able to do a lot. We spent most of our time there in Dotonbori (Time's square of Osaka). Dotonbori was very interesting, but packed with people (a little overwhelming).
