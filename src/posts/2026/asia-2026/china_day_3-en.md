Today is the first test! I volunteered to be on the committee for answering any question about one of the problems. The way this works is that students would ask a question about the problem, then the leader of their country would bring the question to us, together we determine if we are allowed to respond to the question and if so what the answer should be. This is done so that we can answer clarifying question about problem statements without giving away anything related to solving the problem. It was fun, but quite hectic.

After the Q&A portion of the test, we took a break! We had a few choices for the activities and I chose to go to Disneyland. Here is why: 10 years ago I participated in this same competition as a student, in Hong Kong, and we also went to Disneyland. In 2016, the Hong Kong Disneyland was celebrating their 10th year anniversary. This year, the Shanghai Disneyland is celebrating their 10th year anniversary. I could not pass on saying that I got to do this again!
