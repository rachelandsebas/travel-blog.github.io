Sebastian here!

We planned to visit Tianamen Square and Forbidden City today. We had tried to book tickets ahead of time through different means and failed to do so. Blogs we had read said that usually foreigners are allowed to buy them day off. We were unsuccessful at attempting to do this at Tianamen Square, but this seemed to work just fine for Forbidden City. You just have to sign a paper saying that it wasn't possible for you to book ahead (for us, we don't have a mainland China phone number which is required for some apps).

# Forbidden City

Emperors had a good life. This place was huge. We spent 5 hours here and were not able to see everything.

# Haidilao

We went here for dinner. This is a big hot pot chain and we actually have a branch in Toronto, but I haven't taken Rachel there yet, so what better than being in Beijing to do so. We had a great time and they even had us test some new cuts of beef that are not yet in the menu and do some surveys. I think they want foreigners opinions on dishes. One of their survey questions was: "Was any of these two soup bases spicy for you? They are not spicy at all, right?" to which I answered that first one wasn't but I did think the second one was, lol.
