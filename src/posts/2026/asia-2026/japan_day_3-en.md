# Breakfast

For breakfast we went to a 7 Eleven across the street from our hotel. We got smoothies. They have a cool system for it: you buy a cup of frozen fruit, and there is a machine that blends the fruit and adds yogurt. We also got a chocolate croissant and onigiri.

While walking to our first activity, we stopped and got a fruit sando (fruit sandwich). It's whipped cream and pieces of fruit in between bread. It was refreshing and delicious.

# Kiyomuzu-dera Temple

Kiyomuzu-dera is a buddist temple up on a hill just at the edge of the city. A minute after we bought the ticket, there was a clap of thunder and the rain started pouring. We dashed for cover, but thankfully the worst of the rain was over in 3 or 4 minutes. It's impressive how quickly the weather changes.

Then we explored the temple area. It's a gorgeous area with beautiful views of both the mountains and the city. It was also interesting because this is a temple where people actually go to workship as supposed to many that we saw in China that were more historical sites.

# Fushimi Inari

In the afternoon we visited Fushimi Inari a mountain pathway with over 10000 Toriis (gates) and many shrines. It was a steep hike and with the scorching sun, we didn't make it all the way to the top, but still made it to the lookout points. I really enjoyed being in the forest.
