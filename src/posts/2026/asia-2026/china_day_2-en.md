Approving the marking schemes and finishing the translations has been a lot of work. Very long days and very late nights, but we are very happy with the test!

# Opening Ceremony

We travelled to downtown Shanghai for the IMO kick-off! Having been trapped in the hotel for the last few days, this was a wake up call to understand how hot Shanghai is. We got off the bus and it was 38 degrees and felt like 48 according to our weather apps. It felt awful even though it was just a walk from the bus to the venue.

The opening ceremony is always exciting. There were great speeches and really cool art performance, but the coolest thing is seeing 120 countries and 680 students from all over the world. They are all really excited and proud to show their countries on the stage.
